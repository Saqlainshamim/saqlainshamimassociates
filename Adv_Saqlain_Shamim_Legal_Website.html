About the Advocate

Principled advocacy. Rigorous preparation.

Adv. Saqlain Shamim is an Advocate practising before the Supreme Court of India, with a primary focus on criminal litigation and Supreme Court practice.

The practice encompasses legal research, drafting, litigation strategy and representation in matters involving criminal law, personal liberty and related proceedings.
